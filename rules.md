# Rules

## Keep posts relevant to Edinburgh

Posts relating to or about Edinburgh, Scotland and its people.

## 2 If a sticky exists for a subject, use it

There is a permanent Tourism Sticky and rotating Major Event stickies (Fringe, Christmas, World Cup etc).

## 3 No Hateful or Harassing Behaviour

Posts or comments that harass or insult individual posters, or that are racist, sexist, or target people based on their gender, religion, sexuality, national or ethnic origin, are not tolerated. This list is not exhaustive. Generally don't be a cunt.

## 4 Blue light nosiness may be removed

Posts about ongoing incidents which speculate about the nature of the incident or identify individuals involved in an incident may be removed, especially ones that are just curtain twitching nosiness "whats going oan/people huv tae know" and rampant speculation.

## 5 No tourist reviews

Thanks for visiting, haste ye back etc. but keep reviews to TripAdvisor and other review sites.

## 6 No editorialised titles

When posting an Edinburgh related news article, do not edit the title of the article before posting. This can lead to the title "biasing" users opinions. Keep your opinion to the post, keep the title exactly as it was posted.

## 7 No politics except for posts that are directly relevant to Edinburgh

Specific Edinburgh Issues, not general Scottish issues, not UK wide or European issues.

## 8 No advertising, Crowdfunding, AI Pish or Irrelevant Surveys

No buying or selling, no "come see my one man show" etc. Free tickets to gigs etc are allowed, within reason. No crowdfunding/charity collection posts without prior moderator approval. Links to research surveys are permitted only if the subject directly relates to Edinburgh or if the researcher is a member of an Edinburgh educational institution.

Posts or comments composed by AI (yes, we all know) will be removed.

## 9 Low Effort posts are subject to removal

This includes but is not limited to "Where to get a good kebab" type posts, "Look at this picture of [standard tourist attraction]", "What was that loud bang in the Grassmarket" type nosiness, any post where you've done NO work at all "I want to book a Christmas party for 40 people, someone tell me all the venues that exist", any repeat posts where a 30 second search would give you an answer, Missed Connections type posts, Tourist Reviews. Again, not exhaustive.
