# Bakeries

Edinburgh has many bakeries to choose from throughout the city. Although Greggs is prevalent throughout the city we are blessed with several superb alternatives. This is not an exhaustive list and may be missing your favourite one - if so, [let us know](mailto:reddit@cockjuggling.uk?subject=wiki) and we'll update our wiki.

## Sweet and Savoury Bakeries

### Central

* [Twelve Triangles](https://twelvetriangles.co.uk/) - Multiple locations.
* [Preacher's Patisserie](https://www.instagram.com/preacherspatisserie/?hl=en) - Lady Lawson Street
* [Jeannie's Bakery](https://www.instagram.com/jeanniesbakeryedinburgh) - Morrison Street.
* [Room & Rumours](https://www.instagram.com/roomrumourscoffee) - East Market Street

### Bruntsfield / Morningside

* [La Barantine](https://www.labarantine.com/) - Bruntsfield.
* [La Matinée](https://la-matinee.com/) - Morningside

### Stockbridge/ Canonmills

* [Bearded Baker](https://www.instagram.com/beardedbakerscot/?hl=en) - Rodney St.
* [Lannan Bakery](https://www.lannanbakery.com/) - Hamilton Place.

### Newington

* [The Wee Boulangerie](https://theweeboulangerie.co.uk/wp/) - South Clerk Street.
* [101 Bakery](https://www.101bakery.com/) - 101 Newington Road.
* [Considerit](https://www.consideritchocolate.com/) - Sciennes.

### Leith

* [Krema Bakehouse](https://instagram.com/kremabakehouse) - Leith Walk.
* [Sicilian Pastry Shop](https://www.thesicilianpastryshoplimited.co.uk/) - Albert Street, Leith.
* [The Pastry Section](http://www.pastrysection.com/) - Great Junction Street.
* [Baby Faced Baker](https://www.babyfacedbaker.com/) - Leith Walk & Portobello.
* [Storries](https://www.facebook.com/p/Storries-bakery-leith-walk-100067587771973) - Leith Walk - 24 hour bakery - an institution for those that live in Leith.
* [Ante](https://www.instagram.com/ante_coffee/?hl=en) - Haddington Place.
