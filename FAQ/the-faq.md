# Edinburgh Frequently Asked Questions

## I'm not feeling well, who should I contact?

**- If you are seriously ill. Dial 999.**

### Minor ailments

- Supermarkets have numerous non-prescribed medications. These cover a large swathe of common ailments and you don't need to attend your local GP or call 101.
- If you need advice then head to a nearby pharmacist to discuss your ailment and their advice
- If you are feeling unwell and can't get to a pharmacist - call 101 and speak to NHS24
  - Dependant on the time of day this may take a while to speak to a clinician, but advice will be provided and you may be able to get a prescription sent to a nearby pharmacy to collect this. If the clinician believes you need a face to face appointment, they may send you to an Out-patient department or A&E Department. OPD departments will run on a appointments basis. A&E will see you prioritised based on your presentation at your nearby A&E.
