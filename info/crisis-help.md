# Help during a crisis

Within Edinburgh there are a number of organisations geared to helping you during your darkest times. Below are a number of organisations previously discussed and how they can help you when you need them most.

## Check here first

- [This is a comprehensive list](https://edinburghfoodproject.org/free-food-list/) of locations to get a free, or cheap meal to tide you over. These are run by multiple charitable organisations operating across the city.

## Food

- [Empty Kitchens Full Hearts](https://www.emptykitchens.co.uk/)
  - Can provide meals for 4 days of the week and be delivered to you.
  - They'll keep in touch to ensure your well being
  - They provide a community lunch on a Friday from 12pm -> 2pm at 3-11 [West Granton Road, EH5 1HG](https://maps.app.goo.gl/CLyWTfdCsBVgFdh68).

- [Social Bite](https://www.social-bite.co.uk/get-support/#freefood)
  - Provides free food during specific periods of the day
    - Breakfast - 08:30am -> 09:30am
    - Afternoon Scran - 5:00pm -> 6:00pm

- [Guru Nanak Free Kitchen](https://www.facebook.com/gnfkedinburgh/)
  - Every Friday at the Kirkgate in Leith from 5:30pm-6:30pm

## Food Banks

- [Edinburgh City Council](https://www.edinburgh.gov.uk/cost-living/food-bank-information) has a consolidated page detailing all food banks within the city limits.

- [Edinburgh Food Bank](https://edinburghnefoodbank.com/get-help/) - this food bank covers EH6 and EH7 only. A referral is required to gain access to this resource. This can be achieved by calling or emailing them to discuss your circumstances.

- [Tummies not Trash](https://tummiesnottrash.co.uk/) - This operates in the Gorgie area of Edinburgh. Visit their premises at [177 Gorgie Road, Edinburgh EH11 1TJ](https://share.google/lhOnPuR7QJUTNnc9A). **Everyone is welcome.**

- [Goodies](https://goodies.charity/)
  - Providing food distribution from their premises on Cranston Street - [Check for opening times](https://goodies.charity/food-distributions/).
  - Goodies also operates their own "Hub" from their premises at [6A & 6B Grange Road, Edinburgh, EH9 1UH](https://maps.app.goo.gl/vLjZLpnwnhjm7Mk2A).

## Other ways to get a meal

- [Too Good to Go](https://www.toogoodtogo.com/en-gb)
- [Porty Community Fridge](https://actionporty.com/projects/community-fridge/)
  - "Our main aim is to support the local environment by reducing food waste while also helping people in our community who may be experiencing food insecurity."
  - [Contact by Email](mailto://portyfridge@gmail.com)
- [Heart of Newhaven](https://www.heartofnewhaven.co.uk/regular-activities)
  - **Monday 2:30noon - 2pm: Community Lunch (£1)**
    "Free lunch for anyone in the community. No need to book, but please be punctual, and please [contact us](https://www.heartofnewhaven.co.uk/contact-us) if you have allergies or other requirements."

## Advice Centres

- [Citizens Advice Scotland](https://www.cas.org.uk/)
- At risk of homelessness -  call [0808 178 2323](tel://0808 178 2323)  or speak to [Streetwork](https://www.simonscotland.org/get-help/our-support-services/homelessness-in-edinburgh/)
