# Attractions to visit in Edinburgh

Edinburgh has museums, historic buildings and other attractions across the Old Town, city centre and beyond. This page is a reference list, not a recommendation site; check the linked venue pages for current opening times, prices, booking requirements, closures, accessibility information and travel advice before setting out. For reviews, see [Tripadvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/) or Google reviews.

## Royal Mile Sights

* [Edinburgh Castle](https://www.edinburghcastle.scot/) - A hilltop fortress with museums, historic buildings and views across the city. It is at the top of the Royal Mile; allow for a steep uphill approach. Check [tickets](https://www.edinburghcastle.scot/plan-your-visit/tickets/), [opening times and getting here](https://www.edinburghcastle.scot/plan-your-visit/), and the [access guide](https://www.edinburghcastle.scot/plan-your-visit/access-guide/).
* [Palace of Holyroodhouse](https://www.rct.uk/visit/palace-of-holyroodhouse) - The King's official residence in Scotland, at the foot of the Royal Mile, with historic State Apartments and the ruins of Holyrood Abbey. It can close for royal events, so check [tickets and availability](https://www.rct.uk/visit/palace-of-holyroodhouse#book_tickets) and the [mobility access guide](https://www.rct.uk/visit/palace-of-holyroodhouse/mobility-access-at-the-palace-of-holyroodhouse#/).
* [Museum of Edinburgh](https://cultureedinburgh.com/our-venues/museum-of-edinburgh) - Free museum of the city's history in a historic Canongate building. The venue currently lists Wednesday-Sunday, 10am-5pm, with last entry at 4:30pm; check the [museum page](https://cultureedinburgh.com/our-venues/museum-of-edinburgh) before visiting.
* [Museum of Childhood](https://cultureedinburgh.com/our-venues/museum-of-childhood) - Free museum exploring childhood through toys, games and related collections. On the Royal Mile; see the [venue page](https://cultureedinburgh.com/our-venues/museum-of-childhood) for current visitor information.
* [The Real Mary King's Close](https://www.realmarykingsclose.com/) - Guided tours through preserved historic closes beneath the Royal Mile. Advance tickets are available; check [prices and opening times](https://www.realmarykingsclose.com/plan-your-visit/opening-times-prices/), [know before you go](https://www.realmarykingsclose.com/know-before-you-go/) and [access information](https://www.realmarykingsclose.com/plan-your-visit/access-for-all/).
* [St Giles' Cathedral](https://www.stgilescathedral.org.uk/) - A historic church on the High Street, known for its crown spire and Thistle Chapel. Entry is by donation (a £5 donation is suggested); check [visitor information and opening times](https://www.stgilescathedral.org.uk/visitor-information) and the [access guide](https://www.stgilescathedral.org.uk/access).
* [Camera Obscura & World of Illusions](https://www.camera-obscura.co.uk/) - A hands-on attraction of optical illusions and city views, close to the Castle on Castlehill. Check [tickets](https://www.camera-obscura.co.uk/view-tickets/), [opening hours](https://www.camera-obscura.co.uk/opening-hours/) and the [accessibility guide](https://www.camera-obscura.co.uk/accessibility/).
* [Scottish Parliament](https://www.parliament.scot/) - Visit the public areas, book a guided tour or watch parliamentary business at the foot of the Royal Mile, opposite Holyroodhouse. Visits are free; check [opening times, travel and booking details](https://www.parliament.scot/visit/plan-your-visit) and the [accessibility guide](https://www.parliament.scot/visit/plan-your-visit/accessibility-guide). Lothian Buses service 35 stops nearby.
* [Gladstone's Land](https://www.nts.org.uk/visit/places/gladstones-land) - A restored 17th-century merchant's house on the Lawnmarket, with furnished rooms that illustrate life in the Old Town. Check [entry prices and visitor information](https://www.nts.org.uk/visit/places/gladstones-land#entry-prices) before visiting.

## Walking Tours

* [Mercat Tours](https://www.mercattours.com/) - Guided Old Town tours covering Edinburgh's history, stories and underground spaces; many start near the Mercat Cross. Check the operator's site for tour options, prices, meeting points and accessibility information.
* [Edinburgh Free Walking Tour](https://edinburghfreetour.com/) - Walking tours of the city, including [ghost](https://freeghosttour.com/), [New Town](https://freenewtowntour.com/) and [Harry Potter](https://freeharrypottertour.com/) themes. Check the tour pages for schedules, booking and payment details. Old Town streets and historic routes can include slopes, steps and uneven surfaces.

## Other Notable Visitor Attractions

* [National Museum of Scotland](https://www.nms.ac.uk/national-museum-of-scotland) - Large collections spanning Scottish history, science, technology, design and world cultures. Free entry; some exhibitions are ticketed. At Chambers Street in the Old Town; currently open daily, 10am-5pm. See [plan your visit](https://www.nms.ac.uk/national-museum-of-scotland/plan-your-visit) for access and other visitor information.
* [Greyfriars Bobby](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greyfriars_Bobby) - Outdoor statue of the famous dog on Candlemaker Row, near Greyfriars Kirkyard. Free to see and about a five-minute walk from the Royal Mile; there is no ticket or fixed visiting time.
* [Royal Yacht Britannia](https://www.royalyachtbritannia.co.uk/) - Tour the former Royal Yacht, now berthed at Ocean Terminal in Leith. The tram and local buses stop nearby. Check [tickets and tour options](https://www.royalyachtbritannia.co.uk/visit/britannia-experiences/), [opening hours](https://www.royalyachtbritannia.co.uk/visit/opening-hours/), [directions](https://www.royalyachtbritannia.co.uk/visit/find-us/) and [accessibility](https://www.royalyachtbritannia.co.uk/visit/accessibility/).
* [Scottish National Gallery of Modern Art](https://www.nationalgalleries.org/visit/scottish-national-gallery-modern-art) - Modern and contemporary art across two gallery buildings, with a sculpture garden, west of the city centre near Dean Village. Admission is free; some temporary exhibitions may be ticketed. Check the gallery page for current hours, travel and access information.
* [Scottish National Portrait Gallery](https://www.nationalgalleries.org/visit/scottish-national-portrait-gallery) - Portraits and Scottish history in a landmark building on Queen Street in the New Town. Admission is free; some temporary exhibitions may be ticketed. Check the gallery page for current hours, travel and access information.
* [Scottish National Gallery](https://www.nationalgalleries.org/visit/scottish-national-gallery) - Scottish and international art at The Mound, between Princes Street and the Old Town; a short walk from Waverley Station and close to city-centre buses and trams. Admission is free; some exhibitions may be ticketed. Currently open daily, 10am-5pm; see the [visit page](https://www.nationalgalleries.org/visit/scottish-national-gallery) for access and travel information.
* [Edinburgh Zoo](https://www.edinburghzoo.org.uk/) - Wildlife park and conservation charity on a large, wooded hillside in Corstorphine, west of the city centre. Book [tickets](https://www.edinburghzoo.org.uk/visit/tickets) in advance and check [opening hours](https://www.edinburghzoo.org.uk/visit/opening-hours) and the [accessibility guide](https://www.edinburghzoo.org.uk/visit/accessibility); the sloping terrain may affect some visits.
* [Scott Monument](https://cultureedinburgh.com/our-venues/scott-monument) - Victorian monument to writer Sir Walter Scott in Princes Street Gardens. The climb is by stairs only; check the [venue page](https://cultureedinburgh.com/our-venues/scott-monument) for current admission, opening times and visitor information.
* [The Edinburgh Dungeon](https://www.thedungeons.com/edinburgh/) - A theatrical, actor-led attraction presenting stories from Edinburgh's past. Check the official site for show times, ticket prices, booking and accessibility details.
* [Royal Botanic Garden Edinburgh](https://www.rbge.org.uk/visit/royal-botanic-garden-edinburgh/) - A large garden and living plant collection about a mile north of the city centre. Entry to the garden is free; some attractions, including the Palm Houses, require a ticket. The East Gate is served by buses 8, 9, 23 and 27; the West Gate by buses 24 and 29. See the [visitor page](https://www.rbge.org.uk/visit/royal-botanic-garden-edinburgh/) for directions, opening times, tickets and the [access guide](https://www.rbge.org.uk/visit/royal-botanic-garden-edinburgh/access/).
* [Dynamic Earth](https://dynamicearth.org.uk/) - Interactive exhibits about Earth, climate and the natural world, beside Holyrood Park and the Scottish Parliament. Check [plan your visit](https://dynamicearth.org.uk/plan-your-visit/) for current tickets, opening times, travel and accessibility information.
